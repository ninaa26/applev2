How to label

Dashed boxes are the models' guesses that nobody has checked. Every box counts as done only when a person has looked at it: press Enter if the guess is right (it moves on to the next one), or press the right number.

Pass 1: what is it?

  • 1 moth: any moth, even worn, flattened or half under another insect.
  • 2 other insect: flies, wasps, beetles, lacewings, spiders… anything alive-ish that isn't a moth.
  • 3 debris: on the card but not an insect: loose legs, wing bits, scales, leaf pieces, glue blobs. Keep these, don't delete them: the trap sees them too.
  • Delete: only for boxes on nothing at all, or off the card.

Box every insect

Tiny, blurred, half-hidden, overlapping and decayed ones too. A detector trained on photos where they were left out learns to miss them. Press B and drag to draw a box, or in draw mode just click an insect and the site draws the box for you. One box over two insects: shrink it to one and draw the other.

Tags on a box

O (overlapping another insect) is added by itself whenever two boxes share part of their area. Yours to set: E cut off by the photo edge · W worn or decayed · U unsure (you labelled it but aren't confident). Use U rather than guessing.

Pass 2: species (anyone, when you're sure)

Switch to pass 2 on the right. Tab jumps to the moths that have no species yet. CM, OFM, OBLR, the look-alikes (redbanded leafroller, lesser appleworm), other tortricids, other moths. 0 = moth, species unsure. When in doubt, use 0: a wrong species is worse than none, and the examples below show what each looks like on glue.

Comments

Right-click anywhere on the photo to pin a comment (a reviewer uses this to ask for a fix; you can use it to ask a question). Fix what it says, then press Resolve on it.

When you're done

Press Done with this photo. A reviewer accepts it or sends it back with comments.
