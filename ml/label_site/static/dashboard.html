People

Hidden test photos are scored once the reference labels are finished: found = share of the reference's insects they boxed; right = share of those with the reference's label; kept wrong guess = of the boxes where the model was wrong, how often they kept its label (pressing Enter without looking); species right = of the moths both they and the reference named a species for, how many match. Speed is the median time between box changes.

Assignments

Who has which photo. Move gives an open photo to someone else: on a normal photo what was done stays with the photo; a two-person or hidden-test copy starts fresh for the new person, so it can only move before its owner starts it. Pause stops someone getting new photos; they keep and can finish the ones they have. Resume lets new ones come again. While someone is paused, Hand out their photos gives their open ones to whoever has least left.
